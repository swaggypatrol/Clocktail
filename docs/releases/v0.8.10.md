The curve editor's "Observed V/F" layer judges what to draw by time per day of use, and its totals no longer depend on what is drawn.

- **"Ran for" counts everything.** A curve point's "Ran for" and "most often" add up every bin near it, drawn or not. In 0.8.9 they summed only the drawn bins and could read 60 s for a point the card ran at for 220 s.
- **A floor that does not drift.** A bin is drawn when it holds, on average, at least 1 s for each local calendar day with observed time (60 s over 60 days of use). With the same use, what is drawn stays the same as the history grows. Looks that only pass through never pile up past the floor, and hours of heavy use do not raise it. 0.8.9's floor of 0.05 % of all time could reach 18 minutes and hide a region the card really ran in.
- **The window says what it shows.** Its details line reads how many bins are drawn and how much of the observed time they hold.
- **Old evidence is checked too.** `curve_editor` reports what reading the trials' history met (`observed.evidence_read`), which can reach back past the 60 days, beside the haze's own `observed.read`.

The floor starts at 1 s and rises a second with each day of use, so some faint dots from the first days fade out over the next few. That is the rule settling; nothing is deleted.
