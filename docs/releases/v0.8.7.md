The broker now keeps an always-on, lightweight V/F history, and the curve editor and `data_points` read it.

- **Always-on V/F history.** Whenever the broker runs, with or without an agent connected, each 250 ms flight-recorder look whose measured NVVDD is above 825 mV is kept as its time, measured voltage (µV) and measured core clock: 12 bytes per look in `vf-history\yyyyMMdd.vfh` beside the GPU's records. No extra GPU reads, no load or session required, so light loads and transitions are kept too.
- **Safe to leave running.** Writes are batched every 2 s off the sampler, bounded in memory, retried after disk errors without duplicating records, and torn records are cut away. Looks from a hang the flight recorder recognizes, or that a driver reset ends, are withheld. `ping` reports the recorder's status as `vf_history`.
- **Observed time, equal standing.** The editor's "Observed V/F" dots (60 days) and `data_points` `by_point` merge this history with the residency samples, weighted by observed time, so overlapping seconds count once. Observations only say where the card ran without crashing then; stable results still come only from a trial's recorded outcome.
- **Trial windows are clipped exactly.** Time inside a trial's window now counts precisely, so a trial's observed seconds no longer reach past its end (a 30 s trial sampled mid-second reads 29.5 s).

Nothing is deleted automatically: day files grow until you remove old ones (at most about 4 MiB per GPU per day). See `docs/vf-history.md`.

Upgrade: `npx -y clocktail@latest setup`, then restart your agents. Your profiles and history remain in place.
