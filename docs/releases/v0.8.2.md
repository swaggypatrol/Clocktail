Measurements you can trust: fixes to what the watches, tests and the service report, after an outside review and a second look at the same code.

**Clock locks and the live watch**
- **A lock is held only when no sample ran off it.** 0.8.1 rounded the off share to a whole percent, so 1 sample in 1000 over a cap read as "held". The verdict now comes from the count (`off_samples`), and the words say "n of N samples".
- **Every lock period keeps its result.** A lock released or changed mid-watch used to drop out of the summary. `clock_locks` in `live_read`/`live_stop` is now one entry per period, and samples taken after a release are not held against the lock that was already gone.
- **Load and hang rules follow this card.** "Under load" starts at 25 % of the power limit in force, at most 120 W. A suspected hang needs power under 45 % of the default limit, at most 250 W, and a real drop from the last window. A 5090 behaves as before; smaller cards are no longer "never under load" or falsely "hung". Suspected hangs are counted apart from the card not answering (`looked_hung`).
- **Frame rates:** the live feed is safe to read while it samples (no frames counted twice, no watch cut short), reads a long backlog 1 MB at a time, and keeps each swap chain apart, so a second window of the same game no longer halves the fps. The run's fps is now the frames under load (`fps_under_load`), with the whole capture beside it (`fps_whole_capture`). A capture that ended says so instead of "no frames", and one session's stop no longer ends another's capture.
- Time under load comes from timestamps; `live_stop` right after a hang answers with the run so far; an unreadable lock record says a lock may be in force instead of reading as none.

**Tests and evidence**
- **stress_test always ends its load.** nvgwls is killed and waited for on every way out and dies with the server, before the measurement lock is given back.
- **judge.lock:** taking over a stale lock can no longer delete a fresh one, two packaged/unpackaged runs cannot both hold it, and a lock briefly open elsewhere reads as busy, never free.
- **Stable time is counted once:** repeated marks of one trial and the samplers of several open agents no longer add up. The curve editor shows the clock a stable test actually ran when it ran below its setting.
- **Crashes stay with their cause:** a crash under a memory, XBAR or power trial no longer moves the core curve's crash edge; `data_points` lists it as `crashes_under_other_trials`. Each trial has an identity, and a run's crash is recorded only against the trial it measured.
- **Memory:** a slowdown is reported as a slowdown, with heat and error retries as the two causes and the re-test that tells them apart. `lab_test`'s memory history compares only runs at the same crossbar clock and driver, and a run stopped by heat is not kept as a reference.

**The service**
- A leftover game recovery no longer reruns the start every 5 s (repeated false crash records).
- A second driver reset while the first is answered keeps its own evidence; a give-back that never lands still puts the crash on record.
- A trial the service gave back at restart cannot be marked stable, and a trial written after the restart is not ended by the start's profile.
- A refusal because a run is measuring is reported as that, not as groups that "stay at stock".

**Confirmations:** `keep_as_daily` and `load_preset` questions now carry a fingerprint of the values, so a yes cannot keep values that changed after it was asked; the power-cap questions name the exact watts written.

Upgrade: `npx -y clocktail@latest setup`, then restart your agents. Setup also updates the background service. Nothing is written to your card by upgrading; your profiles and history stay as they are.
