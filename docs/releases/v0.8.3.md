Every automatic test result goes to the trial it measured.

- **tune_memory's results name their trial.** A sweep lets go of the card before it records a step, and in 0.8.2 its pass ("stable") and its failure ("crash") went to whatever trial was in force by then, one another agent wrote in between too. Each result now carries the identity of the trial the step measured, and the service records it only against that trial. A refused result is said in the sweep's answer, and a refused pass ends the sweep rather than write its next step over the other trial.
- **The service checks a pass's trial too.** "stable" now takes the measured trial's identity as "crash" has since 0.8.2, checked under the same lock as the record. `mark_stable`, which names none, marks the trial in force as before. `tune_uncore`'s candidate crash names its trial as well.
- **judge.lock:** a look at the lock no longer stops its holder from giving it back, and the waits for a lock another process has open are measured in time, not tries, so a busy machine does not run them out early.

Upgrade: `npx -y clocktail@latest setup`, then restart your agents. Setup also updates the background service, which does the checking. Nothing is written to your card by upgrading; your profiles and history stay as they are.
