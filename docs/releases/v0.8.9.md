The curve editor's "Observed V/F" layer draws only where the card actually runs, not every clock it passed through.

- **Significant bins only.** A bin is drawn when it holds at least 1 s of observed time (four looks) and at least 0.05 % of all the observed time in the 60 days. At light load the clock follows the load, not the curve, and jumps by hundreds of MHz between looks, which scattered single 0.25 s looks over almost every clock between 800 and 890 mV. On the first day of real use this kept 166 of 1009 bins, holding 77 % of the observed time.
- **Nothing is deleted.** The V/F history files are unchanged, and `curve_editor`'s `observed` reports `shown_bins`, `hidden_bins`, `hidden_seconds` and the `min_bin_seconds` applied. `data_points` is unaffected.

Upgrade: `npx -y clocktail@latest setup`, then restart your agents. Your profiles and history remain in place.
