Automatic jobs give back only their own trials, and confirmed profiles are checked when committed.

- **A finished sweep leaves another agent's trial alone.** 0.8.3 correctly refused an old step's result, but the sweep's exit still applied the daily profile over whatever trial had started since. Each automatic trial now remembers its job. Cleanup checks that ownership in the service's operation lock before restoring the daily profile; a newer trial stays on the card and on the record. The same protection covers the uncore tuner and a job write whose response was lost. A job cannot write its next candidate over another agent's trial either.
- **No trial identity, no automatic result.** Memory and uncore measurements stop if the service cannot identify the trial they would measure. Automatic passes and crashes never fall back to marking whichever trial happens to be current.
- **Confirmation covers the values actually saved.** `keep_as_daily` and `load_preset` send the complete approved profile fingerprint to the service. It checks the target again under the operation lock, before saving or writing. If values change while a chat, form or dialog confirmation is pending, nothing is kept or loaded; preview and confirm again. The fingerprint includes settings unchanged from the current daily profile too. Update an older service before using these confirmations.

Upgrade: `npx -y clocktail@latest setup`, then restart your agents. Setup also updates the background service, which performs the ownership and profile checks. Upgrading writes no tuning settings to the card; your profiles and history remain in place.
