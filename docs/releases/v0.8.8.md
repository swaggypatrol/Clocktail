The always-on V/F history from 0.8.7 keeps hangs out more reliably and opens the curve editor much faster on a long history.

- **Long hangs stay out of the history.** A hang the flight recorder has seen stays latched for its GPU until the readings move again or a driver reset ends it. Before, a frozen stretch longer than the 60 s window filled the window, read as a steady load, and its frozen looks were written.
- **Resets cover looks in flight.** A reset's withheld span is kept, so a look that was being read when the reset was seen, and arrived after it, is withheld too.
- **Faster history reads.** Day files now only grow in time, and new files record that in the header, so a time range is found by search instead of decoding the whole day. The editor's 60-day "Observed V/F" layer bins about 20 million looks in about 0.3 s instead of about 2.5 s, with far less memory, and a trial window reads only its own seconds. Day files 0.8.7 wrote are still read, in full, as before.
- **Missing is not mistaken for none.** `curve_editor` (`observed.read`) and `data_points` (`vf_history_read`) report files that could not be read and records that were skipped.

Upgrade: `npx -y clocktail@latest setup`, then restart your agents. Your profiles and history remain in place.
