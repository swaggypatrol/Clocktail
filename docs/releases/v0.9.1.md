Fixes to 0.9.0's memory search and Lab schedules.

- **No comparisons over a failure that is not on record.** When a scan step ends in a driver reset the service has not recorded within 30 s, or a crash it refused to record, `tune_memory` no longer goes on to compare candidates: those comparisons write new trials, which could take the unrecorded failure's blame. The job ends with no recommendation and says what to do next.
- **A slowdown is rechecked against what it was measured against.** The recheck now measures the suspect step against the best bandwidth (or lowest latency) step it was judged slower than, not the step before the slowdown began. A lucky reading there is corrected once, instead of leaving `edge_mhz` on a slowdown the recheck had dismissed and triggering a further recheck at the next step.
- **A flat scan keeps the daily offset without comparing it to itself.** When no scanned step measures ahead of the daily offset, it stays as the confirmed result with nothing written; before, four runs compared daily against itself, and a disturbed run could fail the whole job.
- **Schedules without `seconds`.** `phases: "burn:30,solo:60,leap:60"` with `seconds` omitted runs for the durations' sum (150 s) instead of failing against the old 60 s default, in `lab_test` and `clocktail-lab run`. Spaces around `:` are accepted, every phase needs at least 1 s, and `--seconds` with `--profile` must be whole seconds (a clear error instead of a parse exception).

Validated with simulated workloads and broker/measurement-process tests; no new GPU hardware coverage is claimed.

Upgrade: `npx -y clocktail@latest setup`, then restart your agents. Setup installs the MCP server, lab and broker together; profiles and history remain in place.
