Clocktail stops second-guessing your card. The guards that ran it during development are gone, and so is the preset. What's left are the card's own limits, and a handful of checks for values that can't mean anything.

**Why**

- **The guards were for building it, not for using it.** They kept a reference card in one piece while the tools were young. On your card they were somebody else's numbers in the way.
- **The founders preset was one card's settings.** Your card is different. Your card is always different.

**Now**

- **No preset ships.** No default profile either. `load_preset founders` now says there's no saved profile by that name. If you loaded it before, it's still your daily profile: we stopped handing it out, we didn't take it back.
- **No tuning envelope of Clocktail's.** The rail ceiling, the voltage-demand window, the XBAR ratio window, the fan-curve and zero-RPM rules, the fixed-fan floor, the clock-lock bounds, `CLOCKTAIL_MCP_MAX_XBAR_MHZ` and the CLI's 100 mV undervolt cap are gone. NVIDIA decides what it accepts.
- **What it still refuses**, because these are typos, not settings:
  - a rail offset outside the voltage domain the firmware reports (offsets are rounded to the rail's step);
  - a voltage demand past ±1000 mV (µV typed as mV);
  - an XBAR/core ratio of 0, or a driver that answers with an empty control block;
  - a fan band whose stop isn't under its start, or outside 0–120 °C.
- **The fan guard is gone.** A fixed fan duty is no longer taken back at 85 °C. It still goes back when the trial ends or your agent goes quiet.
- **Thermal stops.** stress_test and lab_test stop within 2 °C of NVIDIA's own slowdown point, or at your `maxTempC`. game_test stops only at your `maxTempC`. tune_memory and tune_uncore don't stop for heat. `CLOCKTAIL_MCP_STRESS_MAX_C` is gone. NVIDIA's own protections are still on.
- **More things ask first.** `begin_session`, `load_daily` and `stock` ask you themselves, and `stock` asks even when there's nothing of another tool's to clear. `CLOCKTAIL_CONFIRM=off` now means `chat`: nothing skips the question.
- **The voltage boost isn't rolled back after it lands.** It's read back, and every rail's MAX is shown before and after.
- **The card report's rails row** marks a REL/ALT or VMIN above this card's own stock with ⚠️, and one below it as undervolted. No fixed voltage is the threshold.
- **The manual got shorter.** It explains how to measure your card, not what ours ran at.
- **The README** puts the point up top and folds the rest.

**Known limits**

- With the guards gone, a bad setting reaches the card the way it would from any other tool. Trials are still recorded and undone by `back_to_daily`, `end_session` or a crash; only `keep_as_daily` makes them stay.
- An unattended night now leans on NVIDIA's protections and your `maxTempC`. Set one if you're going to bed.

Upgrade: `npx -y clocktail@latest setup`, then restart your agents. Your card and profiles stay as they are.
