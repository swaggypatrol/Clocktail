The V/F window in English, with history you can clean up by hand.

- **English throughout the editor:** title, buttons, band tabs, legend, tooltips, validation, save progress and errors. Uses Segoe UI, with wrapped summaries and legend entries to fit the longer labels. Existing test names and notes remain as recorded.
- **Right-click a curve point, crash cross or stable/survived evidence mark** to select a historical test. The menu lists each matching test by time, outcome, duration and game; overlapping records and earlier, lower stable tests remain individually selectable.
- **Deletion asks first.** Confirming removes that test's crash and stability evidence at every point it contributed to and recomputes the remaining marks immediately. Curve edits are preserved, GPU settings are not changed, and the deletion persists when the window is reopened. Canceling curve edits does not undo a confirmed history deletion; Ctrl+Z only undoes curve edits.
- **Right-drag still pans.** A right-click opens history; a drag does not open the menu.
- **No journal rewrite:** deletions are stored as per-record markers in `datapoints.jsonl.deleted/` beside the GPU's `datapoints.jsonl`. Ordinary history readers (including `data_points`) omit deleted tests. The original journal and marker metadata remain for audit/recovery; back up both together. Removing a matching marker restores a test.
- **Safety accounting is not erased.** A session's crash budget and game recovery still count the original records. Raw residency samples (the running-point haze), crash traces and tuning logs are not deleted.

Upgrade: `npx -y clocktail@latest setup`, then restart your agents. No history is deleted merely by upgrading or opening the window; your card and profiles stay as they are.
