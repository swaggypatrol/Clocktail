The V/F curve editor takes on a warm palette: charcoal and cream neutrals with a clay accent.

- **Warm, low-glare canvas.** Backgrounds, grid, panels and borders move from cool blue-grey to warm charcoal; text and the draft curve are cream instead of cold white.
- **Clay accent.** Edited points, the transition band, its draggable lines and Save as Daily use a clay orange in place of blue, with matching hover and pressed states.
- **Evidence stays distinct.** Crashes are rose, stable samples olive green, unverified settings gold and historical load a muted sage, so each mark keeps a hue of its own against the accent.

Colors only: the editor's behaviour, the save flow and every GPU write are unchanged from 0.8.5.

Upgrade: `npx -y clocktail@latest setup`, then restart your agents. Your profiles and history remain in place.
